In plain terms

The latest known list compared with the one before it. A “notable absentee” is a player named on at least 6 matchday squads since 2022, 3 of them as a starter, who is not in this list — international retirements are excluded. An absence may be injury, suspension, a rest or a decision: nothing in this data can tell them apart, and the site does not pretend otherwise. The figures say what was true of the player, not what the selector had in mind.
